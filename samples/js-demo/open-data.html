Our open data policy

Our open data policy follows the Dubai Data Policy. We are committed to sharing data openly, with the highest levels of accuracy and transparency.

  • Open by default. We publish all data that is classified as open, unless the law says it must be protected.
  • Accurate and official. We check every dataset before we publish it, and we say how it was collected.
  • On time. We publish on a set schedule and say when the next update is due.
  • Free to reuse. You can use, share and build on our open data, if you name us as the source.
  • Private data stays private. We never publish personal data, and we remove anything that could identify a person.
